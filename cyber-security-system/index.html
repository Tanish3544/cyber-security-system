TOOL 04: POSTURE AUDIT

Personal Computer Security Assessment

Complete this 10-point educational self-assessment to evaluate your personal endpoint protection practices. Receive a transparent posture score out of 100 with personalized recommendations.

Scoring Methodology: Each positive security practice (Yes) earns 10 points. Choosing "Not sure" awards 5 points and generates a prompt to inspect that setting. "No" awards 0 points and highlights an immediate risk area.

TOOL 06: INCIDENT DECISION SIMULATION

Cybersecurity Awareness Scenarios

Explore 5 realistic workplace and student cybersecurity scenarios. Choose how you would react in each situation to learn the safest security protocols.

📧
Scenario 01: Email Phishing Simulation
You receive an email with the subject: "URGENT: Outstanding Semester Lab Fee Invoice - Due in 2 Hours or Course Registration Cancelled". The email contains an attached zip file named Fee_Invoice_Official.zip and is sent from bursar-college-alert@fastmail-secure.net. What is your response?
💾
Scenario 02: Removable Media Security
While walking through the campus computer engineering parking lot, you find a brand new 64GB USB flash drive labeled "Faculty_Salary_Grades_2026.docx" lying on the pavement. What is your immediate action?
🚪
Scenario 03: Physical Access & Tailgating
You swipe your authorized badge to enter the departmental server room. An unfamiliar individual wearing a telecom technician jacket carrying a heavy toolkit approaches and steps in right behind you before the door latches shut. What do you do?
📞
Scenario 04: Voice Phishing (Vishing)
You receive a phone call from an unknown number. The caller states: "Hello, this is Vikram from Campus Network Support. We detect illegal BitTorrent traffic originating from your student account. To prevent immediate suspension, read me the 6-digit OTP code that was just sent to your phone so I can verify your identity." How do you react?
📶
Scenario 05: Public Wireless Eavesdropping
While waiting at a busy airport terminal, you notice an open, unencrypted Wi-Fi network named "Airport_Free_HighSpeed_Wi-Fi" that connects without requiring any password or terms acceptance. You urgently need to check your bank account balance and submit an assignment. What should you do?
